## Translated system error messages

`src/features/terminal/errorPhrases.generated.ts`, generated by `scripts/import-error-phrases.py`, holds short fixed parts of the error messages of the GNU C Library and GNU Bash, as their translators wrote them in the [Translation Project](https://translationproject.org) catalogues and in Ubuntu's language packs (Launchpad translations). They are used only to recognise error lines in terminal output when the system speaks another language. Thanks to those translation teams.

## ssh-key test vectors

`src-tauri/src/tools/keygen/testdata/` holds OpenSSH and PuTTYgen key files from the test examples of the [ssh-key](https://github.com/RustCrypto/SSH) crate (MIT OR Apache-2.0), used only by the tests to check PuTTY `.ppk` compatibility. They are not part of the application.
