# PlexusTerm 0.2.21

Keep the DEV mode Explorer on your workspace while you open other terminals.

## What changed

- **Pin view:** in DEV mode, turn on **Pin view** under the Explorer toolbar to keep the Explorer on that tab's folder and the folders added to its workspace. Opening another terminal (the **+** button, **Open in Integrated Terminal**, a recent folder) or switching tabs no longer replaces the view.
- **Unpin any time:** turn **Pin view** off to see the active tab's folders again; closing the pinned tab, or turning its DEV mode off, unpins it.
- **Option chips:** in DEV mode, **Follow terminal** and **Pin view** show as compact chips in one row, lit with the accent colour while on. Hover them for the full name; they remain keyboard and screen-reader accessible. Outside DEV mode the Follow terminal folder checkbox is unchanged.
- The new options are translated into all ten application languages.

## Downloads

Choose the asset matching your system: Linux amd64/arm64 (`.deb`, `.rpm`, `.tar.gz` or `.AppImage`), or Windows x86_64 (MSI or NSIS installer). Verify downloads against `checksums.txt`.
