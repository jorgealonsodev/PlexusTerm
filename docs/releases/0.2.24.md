# PlexusTerm 0.2.24

A toolbar you arrange yourself, web links that open in your browser, and more detail in the diagnostic log.

## What changed

- **Arrange the toolbar (Settings → Toolbar):** drag any button along the bar, into or out of a drop-down group, or to Hidden; drag a group by its handle to move it. Every action can go anywhere, including those that started inside More and Tools (Telnet, Mosh, Open shared link, the SSH key generator, the port and network scanners, Wake On LAN and the remote monitor). **New group** adds your own drop-down, named in place; deleting a group puts its buttons back on the bar. Clicking a button offers the same moves in a menu, and **Reset** brings back the default toolbar. Shortcuts keep working wherever an action sits, also when it is hidden; the logo and the Settings button always stay.
- **Web links open in your browser:** links such as **How to install the relay** in Settings → Sharing no longer open inside the PlexusTerm window.
- **Diagnostic log:** it now records when a share starts and ends and which terminal it belongs to, and when a terminal has stopped reading its output for 10 seconds because the window is behind (with how much is still to draw), and when it catches up.
- New texts are translated into all ten application languages.

## Downloads

Choose the asset matching your system: Linux amd64/arm64 (`.deb`, `.rpm`, `.tar.gz` or `.AppImage`), or Windows x86_64 (MSI or NSIS installer). Verify downloads against `checksums.txt`.
