# PlexusTerm 0.2.29

A larger app icon, and the branch menu now shows the branch you are on.

## What changed

- **Larger app icon:** the icon's dark square now fills the whole icon, without a border and with less rounded corners, and its x is larger, so it no longer looks smaller than other apps' icons in the dock or taskbar.
- **The branch in use in the branch menu:** clicking the branch in the bar under a DEV mode terminal lists the branch you are on first, marked **(current)**, above the other branches. It cannot be chosen.

## Downloads

Choose the asset matching your system: Linux amd64/arm64 (`.deb`, `.rpm`, `.tar.gz` or `.AppImage`), or Windows x86_64 (MSI or NSIS installer). Verify downloads against `checksums.txt`.
