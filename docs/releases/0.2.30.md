# PlexusTerm 0.2.30

Open a tab in a new window, with its terminals still running.

## What changed

- **Open in new window:** right-click a tab and choose **Open in new window** to take it out of this window and go on with it in a new PlexusTerm window. Nothing restarts: the shell, an AI command-line tool or an SSH, Mosh, Telnet or serial connection keeps running, each terminal shows what it showed before, scrollback included, and what they print during the move is kept for the new window. The tab keeps its split panes, title, colour and DEV mode with its added folders. AI chats and file viewers of the tab close; a shared tab cannot be moved until its share ends. Closing the new window closes its tabs; closing the first window quits PlexusTerm.
- **Steadier tests:** two tests that sometimes failed in CI no longer do; they had found a short moment after a tab or a folder appears in which a shortcut was ignored or the main folder's menu lacked **Remove folder from workspace**.

## Downloads

Choose the asset matching your system: Linux amd64/arm64 (`.deb`, `.rpm`, `.tar.gz` or `.AppImage`), or Windows x86_64 (MSI or NSIS installer). Verify downloads against `checksums.txt`.
