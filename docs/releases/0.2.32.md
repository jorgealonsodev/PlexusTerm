# PlexusTerm 0.2.32

The AI chat and AI suggestions can run on your ChatGPT subscription: sign in with your ChatGPT account, no API key needed.

## What changed

- **ChatGPT subscription as an AI provider:** in Settings → AI, choose **ChatGPT subscription** and click **Continue with ChatGPT**. Your browser opens on OpenAI's sign-in page; approve PlexusTerm and allow it to use your plan, and the account's email shows under **ChatGPT account**. It uses OpenAI's official Sign in with ChatGPT for open-source apps, with a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan.
- **Your plan's models:** **Model** lists the models your plan offers, and the chat header shows the provider as just **ChatGPT**. Requests count toward your plan's usage; **Manage usage** opens ChatGPT Settings → Usage, where you can see and limit what PlexusTerm uses.
- **Private sign-in:** the sign-in is kept in a file of the app config directory readable only by you; its tokens never reach the interface, logs or error messages. It renews itself, and **Sign out** revokes the session at OpenAI.

## Downloads

Choose the asset matching your system: Linux amd64/arm64 (`.deb`, `.rpm`, `.tar.gz` or `.AppImage`), or Windows x86_64 (MSI or NSIS installer). Verify downloads against `checksums.txt`.
