PRIVACY

PlexusTerm has no accounts, analytics or telemetry. It connects only to:

- The servers and devices you connect to (SSH, SFTP, Telnet, Mosh, serial ports and tunnels).

- GitHub (api.github.com), once at start, to check whether a newer release exists. No personal data is sent. Turn it off in Settings > About.

- The AI provider you set up, only when you use the AI features. Each question is sent with the context the chat shows: the operating system, the shell, the working folder, the recent commands of the linked terminal, and the selection or files you attach. Terminal output is never sent. With OpenCode Go it also reads its public model list and models.dev. The AI features stay off until you add a provider and its key.

Full privacy policy: https://plexusterm.xdev.es/en/privacy/
